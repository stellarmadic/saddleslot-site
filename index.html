Trail rides, booked and matched

Stop losing bookings because you are in the barn.

SaddleSlot connects trail-ride barns with riders. Barns list their horses and ride times. Riders pick a spot, sign the waiver once, and are matched to a horse by weight, height and experience.

Stop losing bookings in the barn

Riders book online at any hour. A request doesn't go unanswered because you were mucking stalls or on the phone with a horse.

The right horse, every time

Weight, height and experience decide the match, so a first-timer never ends up on the big horse.

Ready before the ride

Each day, the barn gets a tack-ready list: who is riding, which horse, and what tack it needs.
